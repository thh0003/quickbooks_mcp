QuickBooks Time & Invoice Connector

An internal tool operated by YOUR COMPANY NAME.

This connector lets our team log time worked by employees and contractors against a customer in QuickBooks Online, and create invoices from that customer's unbilled time, from a command-line assistant on the operator's own computer.

Connected

If you arrived here after approving access in QuickBooks, the connection is complete. Return to the terminal: the tool has saved its credentials locally and no further action is needed in the browser.

What it accesses

Customers, employees, vendors, service items, time activities and invoices in the single QuickBooks Online company you authorised, using Intuit's Accounting scope. It does not read payroll, banking or payment data.
